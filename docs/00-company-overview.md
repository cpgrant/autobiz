# Company overview

**Status:** Blueprint approved; market hypothesis unvalidated
**Owner:** Founder
**Review cadence:** Monthly until validation, quarterly thereafter
**Last updated:** 2026-07-28

## Purpose

Help small businesses obtain reliable operational outcomes without building their
own automation teams.

## Initial thesis

Small consultancies and specialist agencies may lose revenue and time because inbound leads
are followed up inconsistently and client onboarding is fragmented across email,
documents, calendars, and internal checklists. Autobiz may provide a managed,
human-supervised service that standardizes and automates those workflows.

This is an unvalidated thesis. The first objective is evidence, not scale.

## Initial customer hypothesis

Owner-led consultancies or specialist agencies with roughly 2–25 people that:

- receive enough inbound enquiries for missed follow-up to matter;
- have repeatable onboarding steps;
- lack dedicated operations or automation staff; and
- can quantify the value of faster response or reduced administrative effort.

The founder selected this segment on 2026-07-28. Selection narrows discovery; it is
not evidence that the problem or offer is valid.

## Initial offer hypothesis

> Autobiz configures and operates a controlled lead-to-onboarding workflow that
> responds quickly, keeps records complete, and escalates exceptions to a person.

Commercial shape to test: fixed setup fee plus a monthly managed-service fee.

## Operating principles

1. Sell an outcome before building a platform.
2. Perform the workflow manually before automating it.
3. Keep humans accountable for consequential decisions.
4. Make every automated action observable, reversible where possible, and auditable.
5. Use the simplest system that satisfies the current evidence.
6. Protect customer data and minimize access by default.
7. Measure customer outcomes, quality, cost, and founder effort.

## Success definition

The first meaningful proof is not a sophisticated agent. It is:

- one narrow customer segment;
- three paid pilot customers;
- a repeatable result customers value;
- positive contribution margin before founder salary;
- a documented workflow with safe exception handling; and
- declining founder minutes per successful customer outcome.

## Current 90-day priorities

1. Build a register of 20–30 interview candidates in the selected segment.
2. Complete the first five structured customer interviews, then 15–20 total.
3. Sell and deliver a manual paid pilot.
4. Record each step, exception, outcome, and unit cost.
5. Build only the software required by evidence from the pilot.

## Open decisions

- Is lead follow-up, onboarding, or another workflow the strongest entry point?
- Which systems must the first integration support?
- What price produces both customer ROI and healthy contribution margin?
- What data sensitivity and regulatory obligations apply to the selected segment?
