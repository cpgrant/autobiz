# Values and strategy

## Mission hypothesis

Make reliable business operations accessible to small firms through carefully
controlled automation.

## Vision

A small company should be able to operate with the discipline and visibility of a
larger organization without inheriting its overhead.

## Values

### Customer outcomes before automation

Automation is valuable only when it improves a result a customer cares about.

### Human accountability

Software and AI may recommend and execute bounded work; a person remains
accountable for strategy, commitments, money, law, and material customer impact.

### Evidence before scale

Customer behavior, paid use, and measured outcomes outrank internal enthusiasm.

### Simple and reversible

Prefer small changes, explicit state, recoverable actions, and minimal dependencies.

### Privacy and security by design

Collect the minimum data, grant the minimum authority, and retain information only
for a defined purpose.

### Transparent operation

Record important inputs, decisions, tool calls, approvals, costs, and outcomes.

### Sustainable economics

Build for positive unit economics and manageable founder workload rather than
growth without control.

## Strategic choices

### We will

- begin with one vertical and one recurring workflow;
- sell a managed outcome before selling software;
- use a human approval queue for consequential actions;
- use standard integrations and configuration before custom development;
- price for measurable value and operational responsibility; and
- convert repeated delivery knowledge into reusable product capability.

### We will not initially

- build a general-purpose automation platform;
- serve regulated or high-risk workflows without specialist review;
- automate payments, contracts, tax submissions, or destructive changes;
- operate many products, customer segments, or acquisition channels;
- promise an autonomous or error-free service; or
- adopt microservices, Kubernetes, or multi-agent complexity without evidence.

## Strategic horizons

| Horizon | Objective | Evidence gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 months | Validate problem and paid offer | Repeated problem + paid pilot |
| 3–12 months | Make delivery repeatable and profitable | 10 retained customers + positive contribution margin |
| 12–24 months | Productize the repeatable core | Lower delivery effort with stable quality |
| 24–36 months | Expand carefully within the chosen vertical | Retention and referral evidence support adjacent workflows |

## Current objectives and key results

### Objective 1 — establish a real customer problem

- Complete 15–20 interviews across no more than two candidate segments.
- Observe at least five real workflows.
- Quantify current cost or lost value for at least five prospects.

### Objective 2 — prove willingness to pay

- Present a bounded offer to at least five qualified prospects.
- Receive at least three proposal requests.
- Close at least one paid manual pilot.

### Objective 3 — establish operational truth

- Record every delivery step and exception.
- Establish a baseline outcome and delivery cost.
- Identify which steps are deterministic, AI-assisted, or human-only.
