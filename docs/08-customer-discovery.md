# Customer discovery playbook

## Objective

Determine whether one narrow customer segment has a frequent, costly, urgent, and
repeatable lead-to-onboarding problem that it will pay Autobiz to solve.

The interview is not a product demonstration. Ask about recent behavior and real
artifacts before discussing the proposed solution.

## Selected segment

The founder selected **small consultancies or specialist agencies** on 2026-07-28.
This is a discovery focus, not market validation.

Prioritize owner-led firms with roughly 2–25 people and a recent lead or onboarding
workflow they can describe. Sub-segments may include technical consultancies,
design/creative agencies, marketing specialists, recruitment specialists, or other
B2B expert-service firms.

Candidate sourcing and the first five interviews are currently **deferred — required**.
Resume them before offer design or product-specific automation.

## Interview screening

Prefer participants who:

- influence the relevant process or budget;
- have handled a new enquiry or onboarding in the last month;
- can show the current workflow and tools; and
- are not personal contacts trying only to be supportive.

## 30-minute interview guide

### Context — 5 minutes

- What does the firm sell, to whom, and how does a new customer normally arrive?
- Who handles enquiries and onboarding today?
- Approximately how many relevant cases occur in a normal month?

### Most recent case — 10 minutes

- Walk me through the most recent enquiry from arrival to its current state.
- Which systems, messages, spreadsheets, or documents were involved?
- Where did someone wait, repeat work, or search for information?
- What happened when the normal process did not apply?

### Cost and priority — 10 minutes

- When was a lead last lost or delayed? What happened?
- How much staff or owner time does one case require?
- What have you already tried to improve this?
- Who would approve spending to fix it?
- What would need to improve for a solution to be worth paying for?

### Commitment — 5 minutes

- May we observe another real case or review anonymized artifacts?
- If a bounded manual pilot addressed this result, who else would be involved?
- What would prevent you from starting a paid pilot in the next 30 days?

Do not ask “Would you use an AI tool?” or “Do you like this idea?” Those questions
produce weak evidence.

## Evidence record

Create one record per interview containing:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Segment and role | No unnecessary personal data |
| Date and interviewer | Audit context |
| Recent case described | Behavioral evidence |
| Current steps and systems | Actual workflow |
| Frequency | Cases per week/month |
| Cost | Time, money, delay, or lost revenue |
| Existing workaround | Evidence of urgency |
| Budget owner | Commercial path |
| Exact customer language | Positioning input |
| Artifacts observed | Stronger than recollection |
| Commitment offered | Follow-up, observation, proposal, or payment |
| Confidence | Low / medium / high with reason |

Store raw notes separately from summaries. Do not place identifiable customer data
in example code, prompts, or public repositories.

## Scoring rubric

Score 1–5 only after the conversation.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | Rare | Monthly | Weekly/daily |
| Economic impact | Negligible | Noticeable staff cost | Lost revenue or major cost |
| Urgency | No action | Exploring options | Active budget/timeline |
| Repeatability | Mostly unique | Common core with exceptions | Highly standardized |
| Access/integration | Difficult/blocked | Possible with effort | Existing API/export/access |
| Risk | Sensitive/high stakes | Manageable controls | Low-risk and reversible |

## Synthesis after every five interviews

1. Cluster problems using the customers' language.
2. Separate reported opinions from demonstrated behavior.
3. Calculate frequency and cost ranges; preserve uncertainty.
4. Compare segment scores and workflow commonality.
5. Record disconfirming evidence.
6. Update the Lean Canvas and product boundary.
7. Decide to continue, narrow, change segment, or stop.

## Validation gate

Proceed to an offer test only when at least eight interviewees describe substantially
the same costly workflow without being led, and at least five allow observation of
real steps or artifacts. Proceed to software expansion only after a paid manual
pilot produces a measured result.
